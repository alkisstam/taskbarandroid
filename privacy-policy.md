# Privacy Policy for TaskBar

Last updated: May 16, 2026

TaskBar ("the app") is a free Android application that displays a system-overlay 
taskbar above other apps.

## Data Collection
TaskBar does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage 
information. All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using 
Android's DataStore and are never sent anywhere.

## Installed Apps
TaskBar queries the list of installed apps on your device solely to populate the 
app grid and taskbar. This list is used only within the app, is never transmitted 
off the device, and is never shared with third parties.

## Permissions
The permissions declared by TaskBar are used exclusively for local device 
functionality:
- SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: display the taskbar overlay
- QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: list installed apps for the app grid
- WRITE_SETTINGS: toggle auto-rotate and auto-brightness
- ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY: toggle Do Not Disturb
- FOREGROUND_SERVICE: keep the overlay running in the background
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: restore the overlay after reboot

No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

## Third-Party Services
TaskBar does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising services.
