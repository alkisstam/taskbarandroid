Privacy Policy for Floating Dock

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Floating Dock ("the app") is a free Android application that displays a system-overlay dock above other apps.

Data Collection

Floating Dock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore and are never sent anywhere.

Installed Apps

Floating Dock queries the list of installed apps on your device solely to populate the app grid and dock. This list is used only within the app, is never transmitted off the device, and is never shared with third parties.

Permissions

The permissions declared by Floating Dock are used exclusively for local device functionality:

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — display the dock overlay above other apps
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — list installed apps for the app grid and dock
  • WRITE_SETTINGS — toggle auto-rotate and auto-brightness
  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY — toggle Do Not Disturb mode
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — keep the overlay service running in the background
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE — required foreground service type on Android 14+
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the persistent notification required by the foreground service
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restore the overlay automatically after reboot
  • CHANGE_AUDIO_SETTINGS — cycle the ringer mode (Normal / Vibrate / Silent)
  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE — draw the overlay above the system navigation bar; handle back gesture, screenshot, and lock screen quick controls

No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

Third-Party Services

Floating Dock does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

Floating Dock does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
