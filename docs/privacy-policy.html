Privacy Policy for TaskBar

Last updated: May 16, 2026

TaskBar ("the app") is a free Android application that displays a system-overlay taskbar above other apps.

Data Collection

TaskBar does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore and are never sent anywhere.

Installed Apps

TaskBar queries the list of installed apps on your device solely to populate the app grid and taskbar. This list is used only within the app, is never transmitted off the device, and is never shared with third parties.

Permissions

The permissions declared by TaskBar are used exclusively for local device functionality:

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — display the taskbar overlay above other apps
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — list installed apps for the app grid and taskbar
  • WRITE_SETTINGS — toggle auto-rotate and auto-brightness
  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY — toggle Do Not Disturb mode
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — keep the overlay service running in the background
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restore the overlay automatically after reboot
  • CHANGE_AUDIO_SETTINGS — cycle the ringer mode (Normal / Vibrate / Silent)

No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

Third-Party Services

TaskBar does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

TaskBar does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
